Project 01

IT Support Platform

A conversion-focused service site for IT support in Jordan. Built to turn visitors into clients — fast load, clear messaging, direct WhatsApp call-to-action.

The Problem

Most IT support sites in Jordan look outdated and bury the contact info. Clients are already frustrated when they land — they need to reach someone in seconds, not hunt through pages.

How I Built It

Pure HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — no frameworks, no dependencies. Designed mobile-first. Every section has one job: move the visitor one step closer to clicking WhatsApp. Copy was written before the layout — the design serves the message, not the other way around.

Tech Stack

HTML CSS JavaScript

What I Learned

Copy drives conversion more than design. The first version had better visuals but weaker text — bounce rate was high. Rewriting the headline alone changed how long people stayed on the page.
