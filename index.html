04 / Common questions

Before we begin.

Clear expectations protect your time, budget, and data.

Do you provide replacement parts?+

We can recommend, source, and install compatible parts. Hardware costs are confirmed separately before purchase.

Can every lost file be recovered?+

No responsible provider can promise that. We assess the device, explain the risk, and recommend the safest next step before attempting recovery work.

Do you visit homes and offices?+

Yes, on-site support is available in Amman by appointment. Many software issues can also be handled remotely.

What kind of AI work do you provide?+

We focus on practical entry-level automation: chatbots, AI assistants, lead handling, task workflows, and n8n integrations. Larger or advanced systems are scoped separately.
